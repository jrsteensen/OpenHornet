# Manufacturing outputs and assembly definition

## Basis

The [existing manufacturing guide](../../MANUFACTURING.md) recommends JLCPCB, usually two layers, 1.6 mm board thickness and usually top-side SMD assembly. It explicitly defers to board-specific instructions. ATX and Backlight Controller specify copper/layer exceptions; UFC Main and Function Select require both-side SMD assembly. The older generation guide already requires outputs to be regenerated after PCB changes. Reproducibility and explicit acceptance below are **new project policy proposed for adoption**.

## Owner-directed manufacturing and sourcing policy

Owner direction recorded 2026-09-11 establishes **JLCPCB as the intended PCB fabricator and SMD assembler**, JLCPCB-available parts for all fitted SMD positions, and **DigiKey or Mouser for through-hole procurement**. Affordable fabrication and assembly are explicit design objectives. These are confirmed project decisions, not inferred historical defaults; the framework's adoption and deviation process still applies.

- **MFG-012:** All PCB designs MUST meet the current JLCPCB fabrication and assembly requirements for the selected service, layer count, copper weight, stackup and assembly process. Record dated official capability references and selected order options in the fabrication profile. Encode applicable limits in KiCad and explicitly check those that cannot be encoded. Electrical, thermal and mechanical requirements may require stricter constraints; vendor minima MUST NOT replace engineering analysis.
- **MFG-013:** All fitted SMD components MUST be available for procurement and assembly through JLCPCB and assigned to JLCPCB assembly. Verify exact MPN/package, JLCPCB part identifier, service eligibility, available quantity and lead time during selection and again before ordering. An LCSC listing or stock code alone MUST NOT establish JLCPCB assembly availability. Do not silently move unavailable SMD parts to builder assembly or mark required parts DNP; qualify a suitable replacement or obtain a documented owner-approved deviation.
- **MFG-014:** Through-hole components MUST have an orderable exact MPN and a verified DigiKey or Mouser source. Provide distributor SKU/link, quantity and dated availability/lead-time evidence in a separate builder-procurement BOM, with installation responsibility and instructions. These positions MUST NOT be accidentally included as JLCPCB-fitted SMD positions or omitted from the complete assembly definition. Through-hole procurement and installation remain separate from the JLCPCB SMD order unless an owner-approved deviation specifies otherwise.
- **MFG-015:** Every fabrication/assembly output set MUST provide a JLCPCB-compatible manufacturing package: Gerber/drill archive, JLCPCB-format SMD BOM with exact part mapping, compatible CPL/placement file, assembly/fabrication instructions and order parameters. Include a complete master BOM, separate DigiKey/Mouser through-hole procurement list and traceability manifest alongside the upload files. Verify current accepted formats, required fields, units, rotations, assembly sides and reference agreement. Review JLCPCB import/placement feedback before order approval; successful upload alone is not manufacturing or electrical approval. Supplemental KiCad/PDF/STEP evidence does not substitute for the upload package. For a design with no fitted SMD positions, record BOM/CPL assembly submission as not applicable rather than inventing placements.
- **MFG-016:** Designers MUST assess fabrication and assembly cost at the intended prototype/build quantity and record the selected approach and material cost drivers. Prefer electrically suitable, readily available JLCPCB Basic parts where they reduce the current quote; compare Extended-part and other procurement/setup charges rather than assuming list price is total cost. Designers SHOULD minimize unnecessary unique parts, premium processes, assembly sides, layer count, tight fabrication features and board area, subject to required function, margins, fit, serviceability and existing validated constraints. Do not reduce required layers/copper or change a qualified part solely for cost without the applicable engineering review. Include PCB, parts, setup/assembly, special-process and through-hole procurement/shipping costs where available; record date, quantities and assumptions, not permanent price limits.

Official starting points checked 2026-09-11: [JLCPCB fabrication capabilities](https://jlcpcb.com/capabilities/pcb-capabilities), [JLCPCB assembly parts library](https://jlcpcb.com/parts). Recheck the current assembly capability and BOM/CPL guidance for the chosen service when establishing the profile and preparing an order. No stock, fee or numerical capability is frozen by this document.

## Requirements

- **MFG-001:** Generate outputs only from an identified, saved, validated source revision/variant with the fabrication profile established. Use KiCad or an identified version of a reviewed export tool. Record options, units, origins, layer set and source hashes. Refill zones and complete applicable checks first.
- **MFG-002:** The fabrication package MUST include every intended copper layer, appropriate mask/silkscreen, board outline, drill/slot data with plating distinction, and any required paste/fabrication drawings. Check that compressed Gerber packages contain the new files and no stale layers or unrelated boards.
- **MFG-003:** Open and inspect the generated CAM itself in a Gerber/drill viewer. Check outline/cutouts, layer count/alignment, holes, copper clearances and connectivity features, polarity, mask openings and unexpected objects. Source screenshots are not CAM review.
- **MFG-004:** Reconcile source, fabrication notes, Gerber metadata and supplier order parameters for layer stack, copper weight, board thickness, finish, tolerances and assembly sides. Never accept supplier defaults or automatic detection without checking. Explicit conflicting instructions block manufacturing approval.
- **MFG-005:** BOM records MUST contain reference designators, quantity, manufacturer/exact MPN or approved equivalent, value/specification, package, supplier code where needed, DNP state and assembly responsibility. Reconcile all populated references with source and distinguish intentionally hand-assembled modules/displays/connectors from omitted parts.
- **MFG-006:** CPL/position records MUST use the supplier's required units, origin, axis/rotation convention and side notation. Verify polarity and pin 1 in supplier placement previews, especially mirrored bottom-side parts. Do not assume KiCad zero rotation equals the supplier's zero rotation.
- **MFG-007:** Review actual supplier-selected parts against the approved BOM, including substitutions, package suffixes and stock-dependent changes. Missing components require resolution under MFG-013/014; DNP is only for intentional assembly variants, and hand assembly of a required SMD part requires an owner-approved deviation. Accepting an automated omission is not a complete assembly definition.
- **MFG-008:** Provide assembly instructions for jumper defaults, termination options, fitted module versions/modifications, displays, connectors, polarity and special handling. Follow manufacturer moisture/bake/reflow limits for the selected parts; the UFC bake note is not a universal bake recipe.
- **MFG-009:** Preserve source→CAM→BOM/CPL→order/prototype traceability with a manifest or review record containing hashes, revision and date. Update the applicable `JLCPCB/production_files` and `release/OHE_Electronics` packages together when preparing a hardware release. Development output may remain provisional, clearly labeled; do not silently present stale release files as current.
- **MFG-010:** A different fabricator, SMD sourcing route or assembly process requires an owner-approved deviation and comparison against the complete fabrication profile and output format. JLCPCB is the intended fabricator/SMD assembler; old capability assumptions and service-tier compatibility still require verification.
- **MFG-011:** Exclude temporary plots, caches, local settings and scratch outputs from new commits. Commit only intended authoritative sources and requested reviewed deliverables. Existing historical output clutter is not authorization for cleanup in a documentation-only PR.

## Prototype order versus release

A manufacturable prototype may be ordered after human review and documented acceptance of prototype limitations. This is not release qualification. Manufacturing review MUST state which exact package was reviewed, remaining supplier questions and any assembly deviations. Hardware release requires [QUALIFICATION.md](QUALIFICATION.md).
